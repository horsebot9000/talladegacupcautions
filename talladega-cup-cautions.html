Method. Caution laps, reasons and car numbers come from the Racing-Reference caution breakdown for each race. Car numbers are matched to drivers through the uploaded lap CSVs.

Catastrophic means the car retired (crash, DVP or a failure) without being caught in a later wreck, within 10 laps of the restart, or fell 3+ laps further behind the leader from the lap before the wreck to the end of its race, measured from cumulative lap times. Exit laps for lapped cars are converted to the leader's lap count before matching.

Adjustments. Cars marked "lap data" were not in the official list but retired with a crash status on the lap of that caution: Chase Briscoe (2022 Spring lap 58), Matt DiBenedetto (2021 Fall lap 117), Ryan Preece and Daniel Hemric (2024 Fall lap 185). #53 James Davison (2020 Fall lap 184) and #00 Quin Houff (2021 Spring lap 173) were kept even though their lap counts are lower, because they were many laps down. Rain, debris, fluid, competition, stalled-car and stage cautions show no squares. The files cover spring and fall 2020 to 2025 plus spring 2026; the 2026 fall race is not included.
